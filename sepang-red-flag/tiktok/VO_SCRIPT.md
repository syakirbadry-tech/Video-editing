# Sepang red flag — voice-over script (English, ~49 s)

Read it straight through in one take, a little faster than normal conversation, with energy on the
**bold** words. Leave a short beat where you see `/`. The times are where each line lands in the
preview edit; you don't have to hit them exactly — the edit gets re-cut to your recording.

**Recording tips:** quiet room (a car or a wardrobe works well), phone 15–20 cm from your mouth,
Voice Memos at high quality. Do 2–3 full takes and upload them as `.m4a` (or `.wav`) to the same
Drive folder as the clips.
