# Sepang red flag — voice-over script (English, ~65 s)

The preview carries an **AI stand-in voice** (`tiktok/voice.py`, Kokoro TTS run locally) so the edit can be
judged with narration. Each line has a delivery — excited, sad, confused — set by punctuation, speed and
a light pitch change. Replace it with your own read whenever you like: the times below are where each line sits in the edit.

Read it as one continuous story: full sentences, no long stops mid-line. **Bold** = punch the word.

**Recording tips:** quiet room (a car or a wardrobe works well), phone 15–20 cm from your mouth,
Voice Memos at high quality. Do 2–3 full takes and upload them as `.m4a` (or `.wav`) to the same
Drive folder as the clips; the edit gets re-cut to your recording.

The music (`tiktok/music.py`) is cut to these moments: it drops on "Malaysia", falls silent on "nothing happened",
hits the MGU-H reveal, goes to piano for "gone for good" and the red flag, and drops again on "we went racing".

Facts in the script come from the red-flag explainer in this folder (`main.js`). The MGU-H facts are the
general ones: it sat on the turbo shaft between turbine and compressor, recovered energy from the exhaust
and could spin the turbo up to kill lag, and the 2026 power-unit rules dropped it. Double-check anything
you're unsure of before posting.
